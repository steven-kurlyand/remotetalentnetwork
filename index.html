ABOUT US

A global software development team

We are a global software development team actively pursuing remote software engineering opportunities in the United States. Our engineers are based across Europe and Asia and collaborate with U.S.-based partners while pursuing senior software engineering opportunities.

We support candidates who are struggling to secure offers, experiencing limited interview schedules, or looking to manage multiple opportunities. Because of the volume of interview requests, we are looking for technically capable professionals with strong spoken and written English who can participate confidently in U.S.-based interviews and client-facing meetings.

ROLE MODEL

Interview Participation and project collaboration

Participants work under our team developers' identity and professional background. The team may provide interview preparation, mock interviews, technical coaching, coding practice, behavioral guidance, and system-design support before or after interviews. Any tools or assistance used during an interview must be permitted by the prospective employer.

01

Interview Participation

Attend recruiter, behavioral, technical, coding, system-design, architecture, and hiring-manager interviews while communicating naturally and confidently.

02

Interview Preparation

03

Post-Selection Collaboration

After selection, participate in daily meetings, communicate project status, discuss tickets and deliverables, and coordinate with the development team responsible for implementation.

HOW THE PROCESS WORKS

From application to ongoing project participation

1. Job Applications

A developer identifies and applies for suitable remote U.S. software engineering positions.

2. Interview Scheduling

Once an interview is scheduled, the participant receives information about the company, role, expectations, and relevant preparation materials.

3. Interview Participation

The participant joins the scheduled interview under one of our developers' identity and presents their own experience accurately.

4. Preparation & Support

The team can provide permitted preparation through AI tools, chat, mock interviews, remote collaboration, coding practice, and technical guidance.

5. Offer & Selection

If a company extends an offer and it is accepted, the engagement moves into the project-support phase.

6. Ongoing Project Participation

Join stand-ups and scheduled meetings, communicate progress, discuss tickets and deliverables, and stay synchronized with the assigned development team.

7. Multiple Opportunities

The process may be repeated for additional opportunities depending on availability, meeting schedules, existing commitments, and capacity.

WORKING HOURS

100% remote

Candidates should generally be available between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM Central Time. Interview schedules may vary depending on the prospective employer. After joining a project, participants follow the client's meeting schedule, with most daily stand-ups taking place during U.S. business hours.

COMPENSATION

Based on involvement and engagements

Interview participation: $1,000 per month base compensation.

Successful job engagements: 25% commission-based compensation per job, estimated in the source description at approximately $2,000–$2,500 per month per engagement depending on position compensation.

Example: Five engagements at approximately $2,500 each would equal approximately $12,500 per month.

SCALABILITY & FLEXIBILITY

Capacity-based participation

There is no fixed maximum number of engagements. Capacity depends on interview schedules, meeting schedules, communication requirements, existing commitments, and personal bandwidth. Long-term collaboration is expected when performance and communication are strong. Participation may occur alongside another project or job when schedules and responsibilities can be managed without conflicts.
